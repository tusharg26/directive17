# Why We Exist

Most organizations begin with markets. **We begin with people.**

Every day, billions of people wake up hoping for healthier lives, stronger relationships, more meaningful work, more joyful experiences, and a better future for their families.

We believe business can help create that future — not by chasing trends, but by building enduring companies that genuinely improve people's lives.

## Our Belief

Progress should improve the human experience.

- Technology should make life more human.
- Food should bring people together.
- Entertainment should create happiness.
- Health should be extended.
- Trust should be earned.
- Communities should grow stronger.
