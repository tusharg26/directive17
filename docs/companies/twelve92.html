Twelve92

Twelve92 is not another private club. It is an entirely new category of hospitality—a private community and lifestyle platform designed to make life more extraordinary, more convenient, and infinitely more enjoyable.

At its heart, Twelve92 is built around a simple belief: Life is better when people come together. In a world increasingly dominated by technology, isolation, and endless demands on our time, Twelve92 brings back something that has become surprisingly rare—genuine human connection, extraordinary hospitality, and the simple pleasure of being taken care of.

Imagine having access to a beautiful private destination where everyone knows your name, remembers your preferences, and treats you like family. A place with exquisitely designed private dining rooms, welcoming lounges, exceptional wines, and remarkable food prepared by world-class culinary talent. Whether you’re entertaining friends, celebrating an anniversary, meeting interesting people, or simply enjoying an evening out, every detail is handled for you.

You become the host. Twelve92 takes care of everything else.

But the physical club is only the beginning.

Twelve92 is designed to become an extension of its members’ everyday lives. Through its extraordinary hospitality team, intelligent technology, and global network of relationships, members gain access to an ever-expanding collection of services, experiences, and opportunities.

Need transportation, a last-minute reservation, a private chef, a weekend getaway, or someone to coordinate an important family celebration? Consider it handled. Want to reconnect with old friends, arrange an unforgettable date night, discover a remarkable wine, or fulfill a lifelong dream? Twelve92 makes it possible.

And sometimes, members won’t even know what to ask for until Twelve92 surprises them.

Rather than defining hospitality through a conventional list of benefits, Twelve92 operates around two extraordinary words: Just Ask.

Our goal is to eliminate the friction between wanting to do something and actually experiencing it. From everyday conveniences to once-in-a-lifetime adventures, Twelve92 becomes the trusted organization members turn to when they want something done beautifully.

Equally important is the community itself.

Twelve92 will bring together fascinating people across generations through curated introductions, extraordinary dinners, cultural experiences, shared interests, celebrations, and spontaneous gatherings. It will help members build friendships, strengthen families, discover new passions, and create memories that last a lifetime.

Technology will quietly make the experience even more personal. Our AI concierge will remember preferences, anticipate needs, coordinate services, and help members discover opportunities. But technology will never replace the human touch. It will make that touch more extraordinary.

The inaugural Twelve92 flagship will be deliberately limited across individual, multigenerational family, and corporate memberships.

This intentional scarcity protects the quality of the experience while creating a genuine community where relationships can flourish.

Twelve92 will have weekly curated experiences, proprietary products, and an expanding universe of lifestyle services. Over time, Twelve92 may extend beyond its original location into a carefully selected network of extraordinary destinations and relationships around the world.

We are not building a better private club. We are redefining what it means to belong, to be cared for, and to enjoy life.

Because the greatest luxury isn’t simply what you own or where you go.

It’s having more time, more meaningful relationships, more unforgettable experiences—and more reasons to smile.

TWELVE92 — A Richer Human Life.
