Caviar & Corndogs

Caviar & Corndogs is a hospitality destination built around extraordinary food, everyday pleasures, discovery, and human connection. Planned for Atlanta’s South Buckhead neighborhood, the approximately 18,000-square-foot flagship brings together dining, a curated gourmet market, an in-house coffee roastery, a bakery, a chocolate room, exceptional wines and Champagne, and beautiful spaces to gather.

The name captures the spirit: caviar represents the exceptional; corndogs represent familiarity, nostalgia, and fun. Both belong at the same table. You should feel equally welcome stopping in for a dollar coffee, picking up something wonderful for dinner, or spending the evening with friends over rare wine and an extraordinary meal.

We call the philosophy Generous Luxury: exceptional quality, thoughtful pricing, warm hospitality, and unexpected generosity.

Every product must earn its place. The market will feature outstanding beef and seafood, carefully sourced poultry, fresh pasta, artisan cheeses, remarkable olive oils, seasonal produce, freshly baked breads, and foods worth discovering. Ingredient integrity and traceable sourcing guide the selection. Guests should know where their food comes from—and why we chose it.

Throughout the space, craftsmanship becomes part of the experience. Coffee roasting behind glass. Fresh bread emerging from the ovens. Chocolate being made and tasted. A beautiful fish selected at the counter and prepared for dinner. Flowers, pastries, wine, and thoughtful gifts make even a quick visit feel like an occasion.

Caviar & Corndogs is designed to become part of everyday life. Come for breakfast, meet someone for lunch, collect a beautifully prepared meal on the way home, or settle in for dinner. Grab-and-go convenience receives the same care as a celebration. The atmosphere is beautiful, lively, and welcoming, with room for families, young professionals, creatives, neighbors, and anyone who enjoys being around people.

Generosity will show up in tangible ways. Our planned daily Champagne and Caviar Hour offers adults a complimentary glass of Champagne and a taste of caviar on a corndog, while children join the celebration with sparkling grape or apple juice. House coffee costs $1, with that dollar donated to charity. Children receive free gelato. Dinner guests leave with a breakfast pastry for the next morning.

Even take-home packaging expresses the philosophy: reusable glass containers, a little humor about bringing them back, and no deposit. We trust our guests.

Behind these gestures is a business designed around frequent visits and complementary purchases across dining, prepared foods, coffee, bakery, wine, specialty groceries, catering, and gifting. Each experience creates a reason to return—and something worth telling a friend about.

Caviar & Corndogs exists to make everyday life more delicious, more beautiful, and more connected.

And, above all, to make people smile.
