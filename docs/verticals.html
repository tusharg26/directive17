The Directive 17 Difference

We are not limited to entertainment, hospitality, technology, sports, or consumer products.

We invest in outcomes that matter to human beings.

We want people to be happier, healthier, safer, more connected, more entertained, and more fulfilled.

Every investment should answer three fundamental questions:

  1. 01Will this business genuinely make people’s lives better?
  2. 02Can we build a scalable, financially successful business around that purpose?
  3. 03Can Directive 17 contribute something meaningful beyond capital?

Our ultimate objective is to generate exceptional financial returns alongside something most investment firms never bother to measure:

The return on human happiness.

And occasionally, we’re going to invest in something so fucking crazy that nobody else would even consider it.

Because sometimes the craziest ideas create the biggest smiles.
